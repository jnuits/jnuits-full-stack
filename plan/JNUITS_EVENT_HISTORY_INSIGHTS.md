## Who JnUITS Is

Jagannath University IT Society, short form JnUITS, is a non-profit university IT club at Jagannath University. The society focuses on practical IT learning, campus skill development, competitions, workshops, digital literacy, member leadership, and community-building programs.

## What JnUITS Has Historically Done

JnUITS has repeatedly organized:

- Skill-development workshops and seminars: Basic Computer Course, CV writing, LinkedIn optimization, graphic design, Odoo ERP, blockchain, digital communications, Excel, public speaking, professional email writing, app development, and presentation skills.

- Student onboarding and leadership flows: orientations, fresher receptions, member recruitment, registration booths, viva/assessment exams, roadshows, executive planning meetings, junior coordinator meetings, and general member programs.
